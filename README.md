## 🚀 Featured Engineering Projects

### 📡 Enhanced-Stability High Frequency PLL Architecture
**Engineering Problem:** Multi-gigahertz frequency synthesis requires exceptionally low timing uncertainty and minimized phase errors.
*   **Approach:** Designed and verified a CMOS PLL for fine-resolution 2.5–3.8 GHz synthesis. Implemented a dead-zone-free PFD with balanced latch paths, a matched charge pump, and a source-degenerated current-starved VCRO.
*   **Technology:** Cadence Virtuoso, Spectre, GPDK090 technology.
*   **Outcome:** Achieved closed-loop locking around 2.506 GHz with 0.160% normalized ripple. Extracted 70.98 fs RMS jitter and 1.588 ps peak-to-peak jitter.

### ⏱️ High-Performance PFDs & VCOs for PLL Applications
**Engineering Problem:** High phase noise and dead-zone behavior in standard PLL configurations limit spectral purity.
*   **Approach:** Engineered a 5-stage Voltage-Controlled Ring Oscillator (VCRO) achieving a wide tuning range of 1.25 MHz to 3 GHz. Optimized the PFD reset path to eliminate dead-zone behavior.
*   **Technology:** 90 nm CMOS, Cadence Virtuoso.
*   **Outcome:** Achieved PFD phase noise of -143.71 dBc/Hz at 1 MHz offset and reduced lock-in time to ~31 ns.
